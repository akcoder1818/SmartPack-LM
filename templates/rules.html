Enforcement & Legal Metrology Act, 2009 Provisions

Under Section 36(1) of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009, whoever manufactures, packs, imports, sells, distributes, delivers, or offers for sale any pre-packaged commodity which does not conform to the declarations under Rule 6(1) shall be punished with fine up to ₹25,000 for the first offence, up to ₹50,000 for the second offence, and up to ₹1,00,000 or imprisonment for subsequent offences.
