Legal Metrology Compliance Rules
Rule 6(1) Standards

The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 mandate seven core statutory declarations on every retail packaged commodity sold in India:

Rule 6(1)(a): Manufacturer / Packer
Rule 6(1)(b): Generic / Common Name
Rule 6(1)(c): Net Quantity in SI Units
Rule 6(1)(d): Month & Year of Mfg
Rule 6(1)(e): MRP (incl. of all taxes)
Rule 6(1)(f): Consumer Care Redressal
Rule 6(1)(g): Country of Origin / Manufacturing
Includes 2021/2022 Unit Sale Price (USP) Amendment View All Rules
How SmartPack-LM Works
4-Step Workflow
1
Capture or Upload Packaging

Upload or take live photographs of the front panel and statutory declarations panel with GPS tag.

2
Automated OCR Extraction

Multi-tier computer vision extracts text tokens, identifies statutory declarations, and maps bounding boxes.

3
Rule Validation & Scoring

Statutory engine evaluates declarations against Rule 6(1), verifying SI metric units, tax clauses, and contacts.

4
Audit Record & Grievance Redressal

Download official multilingual PDF audit report, or 1-click submit grievance for non-compliant products.
