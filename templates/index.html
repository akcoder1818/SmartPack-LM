SmartPack-LM verifies packaged goods across all FMCG, food, retail & e-commerce sectors.
Why SmartPack-LM?
Enterprise-grade AI enforcement of Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011
GovTech AI Stack
AI-Powered OCR

Multi-tier computer vision (PaddleOCR, Tesseract, and regex coordinate extraction) with adaptive CLAHE filtering.

Compliance Check

Automated Rule 6(1) validation of MRP with tax clause, Net Quantity in SI units, Mfg date, Packer & Origin.

Location & Camera

High-precision GPS tagging with reverse-geocoded retail market mapping and live in-browser camera scanning.

Reports & Complaints

Official multilingual PDF audit certificates with QR verification and 1-click citizen grievance redressal.

How It Works
4-Step Inspection Workflow
1
Scan

Capture or upload packaging images (front & back statutory panels) with GPS market location tag.

2
Analyze

Multi-tier OCR extracts text tokens, identifies statutory declarations, and maps bounding box coordinates.

3
Get Result

Statutory rule engine assesses compliance against Rule 6(1) and computes the compliance score.

4
Take Action

Download official multilingual PDF inspection report or file a citizen grievance with evidence in 1 click.
