Rule 6(1)
Mandatory Statutory Declarations for Packaged Commodities
Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 — Every pre-packaged commodity must display these 7 statutory particulars:
View Complete Rules & Thresholds →
Manufacturer / Packer
Complete name and postal address of manufacturer, packer, or importer.
Country of Origin
Mandatory country of manufacture or assembly for all imported packages.
Generic Commodity Name
Common or generic name of commodity contained in the package.
Net Quantity (SI Units)
Net weight/volume in standard metric SI units (g, kg, ml, l).
Date of Manufacture / Packing
Month and year of manufacture, packing, or import clearly declared.
MRP & Unit Sale Price
Maximum Retail Price inclusive of all taxes, plus unit sale price where mandated.
Consumer Care Helpline
Official name, address, telephone number, and email for consumer grievance redressal.
